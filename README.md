# Offer Simulator

Break-even analysis for DTC offers. Set up a control (the offer you run today) and up to seven variants, then see for each one:

- the ROAS it needs to break even
- the ROAS and conversion rate you expect
- how far conversion can fall before it loses money
- the contribution profit and margin it leaves each month, after ads

Ad spend and cost per click stay fixed, so the only question is which offer leaves the most money at the conversion rate you expect.

## Using it

- **Paste From AI** (top right) fills the whole simulation in one go. Copy the instructions, paste them into ChatGPT or Claude with your store numbers and offer ideas, then paste the reply back in.
- **Copy Link** saves the whole simulation inside a link. Nothing is stored on a server.
- **Print** from your browser (or Save as PDF) gives a one-page record of the prediction.

Everything runs in the browser. Your numbers are kept only in your own browser's storage.
